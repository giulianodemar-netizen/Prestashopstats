DESCRIPTION
-----------
Comprehensive statistics module for PrestaShop providing detailed analytics 
about sales, customers, products, geolocation, and traffic.

FEATURES
--------
✓ Sales Statistics
  - Total orders and revenue tracking
  - Sales breakdown by product
  - Sales breakdown by customer
  - Sales trends by date

✓ Customer Insights
  - Total active customers
  - New customer acquisition
  - Geographic distribution
  - Purchase frequency analysis

✓ Product Metrics
  - Most purchased products
  - Product view tracking
  - Category performance

✓ Geolocation Data
  - Orders by country
  - Traffic by country

✓ Traffic Analytics
  - Visit tracking
  - Page view counting
  - Conversion tracking
  - Traffic source attribution

✓ Dashboard Integration
  - Comprehensive statistics dashboard
  - Date range filtering
  - Responsive design
  - Real-time data

COMPATIBILITY
-------------
PrestaShop: 1.7.8.0 - 9.x
PHP: 7.1+
MySQL: 5.6+
