Database Tables Created:
========================
1. ps_prestashopstats_page_views
   - Tracks product and page views
   
2. ps_prestashopstats_traffic
   - Tracks traffic sources and origins
   
3. ps_prestashopstats_conversions
   - Tracks order conversions

Hooks Registered:
=================
1. displayBackOfficeHeader
   - Loads admin CSS and JavaScript
   
2. displayAdminStatsModules
   - Displays dashboard widget
   
3. actionCustomerAccountAdd
   - Tracks new customer registrations
   
4. actionValidateOrder
   - Tracks order conversions
   
5. actionObjectProductUpdateAfter
   - Tracks product updates

Features by Section:
===================

Sales Statistics:
- Total orders count
- Total revenue sum
- Top 10 products by revenue
- Top 10 customers by spending
- Daily sales trends

Customer Insights:
- Total active customers
- New customers in period
- Customers by country
- Purchase frequency segmentation

Product Metrics:
- Most purchased products
- Product view counts
- Sales by category

Geolocation Data:
- Orders by country
- Traffic by country

Traffic Analytics:
- Total visits
- Page views
- Conversion rate
- Traffic sources
- Daily traffic trends

All features include date range filtering!
